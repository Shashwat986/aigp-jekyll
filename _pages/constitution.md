# Constitution And Bylaws

This page is reserved for the official AIGP constitution, bylaws, and governance documents.

Until a full formal document is published here, AIGP operates with the following public principles:

- Promote Go, Baduk, and Weiqi across India.
- Support players of all levels through events, learning resources, and community activity.
- Maintain fair and transparent tournament and selection processes.
- Represent Indian Go players in international forums and competitions.
- Keep official decisions discoverable through the [Official Notices](/notices/) page.

For governance questions, use the [Contact](/contact/) page or the [Contact and Escalation Policy](/contact-escalation/).
