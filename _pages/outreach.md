<section class="site-section--tight">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <span class="section-eyebrow">Teaching Kit</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">What A Beginner Session Needs</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="card-grid card-grid--3">
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">Short Intro</h3><p class="official-card__text">Explain liberties, capture, territory, and passing. Keep ko and scoring details light until students have played a few games.</p></div></div>
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">Small Boards First</h3><p class="official-card__text">Use 5x5, 7x7, and 9x9 boards so new players finish games, see captures, and understand territory quickly.</p></div></div>
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">Follow-up Route</h3><p class="official-card__text">Give learners links to AIGP lessons, the community page, beginner-friendly online play, and a local club or repeat session if available.</p></div></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="site-section--tight">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <span class="section-eyebrow">Youth And Schools</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">Guidance For Parents, Teachers, And Organizers</h2>
    <p class="section-summary">AIGP does not currently operate a staffed youth programme. We can, however, help schools, colleges, parents, and local organizers plan welcoming beginner sessions and find an appropriate route to continue playing.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card-grid card-grid--3">
    <article class="official-card">
      <div class="official-card__body">
        <h3 class="official-card__title">Request A School Or College Workshop</h3>
        <p class="official-card__text">Use the contact form to share the institution, age range, expected group size, city, preferred dates, language needs, available boards, and whether teachers or parents will be present. AIGP will respond where a suitable volunteer or organizer is available.</p>
        <p class="mt-5"><a class="button button-secondary" href="/contact/">Request A Workshop</a></p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="official-card">
      <div class="official-card__body">
        <h3 class="official-card__title">Start Small And Continue Locally</h3>
        <p class="official-card__text">Begin with short games on 5x5, 7x7, or 9x9 boards, then offer a regular club time, a teacher or organizer contact, and a clear follow-up route through AIGP lessons, community groups, or a local session where available.</p>
        <p class="mt-5"><a class="button button-secondary" href="/learn/">Browse Learning Resources</a></p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="official-card">
      <div class="official-card__body">
        <h3 class="official-card__title">Parents And Guardians</h3>
        <p class="official-card__text">Parents and guardians can use the contact form for questions about a workshop or local activity. Everyone involved should follow AIGP's conduct expectations, and concerns can be raised through the contact and escalation route.</p>
        <p class="mt-5"><a class="button button-secondary" href="/code-of-conduct/">Read Code Of Conduct</a></p>
      </div>
    </article>
  </div>
  <div class="official-card official-card--flat mt-5">
    <div class="official-card__body">
      <h3 class="official-card__title">Photos, Names, And Results</h3>
      <p class="official-card__text">Before submitting photos or identifying information about children for AIGP channels, the organizer must obtain the appropriate parent/guardian and institution permission. Do not send private contact details. AIGP will not publish a youth calendar, standings, participant profiles, or identifying results until there is an operating owner and a documented consent and safeguarding process.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
