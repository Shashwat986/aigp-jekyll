    <h1 class="section-title">Contact And Escalation Policy</h1>
    <p class="section-summary">Use the lightest appropriate route first. General questions can go through the contact form; event, eligibility, selection, conduct, or correction concerns need enough detail for review.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-3 gap-5 my-8">
  <section class="official-card lg:col-span-2">
    <div class="official-card__body">
      <span class="official-card__meta">What To Include</span>
      <h2 class="official-card__title">Make the issue easy to verify</h2>
      <ul class="mt-5 space-y-2">
        <li>Your name and contact email.</li>
        <li>The event, page, tournament, club, rating, selection, or issue being referenced.</li>
        <li>Relevant dates, links, screenshots, pairings, standings, or game records when applicable.</li>
        <li>The organizer or channel you already contacted, if any.</li>
        <li>Whether the matter is urgent, sensitive, private, or child/youth related.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>
