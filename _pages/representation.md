    <span class="section-eyebrow">Official Pathway</span>
    <h1 class="section-title">National Representation</h1>
    <p class="section-summary">AIGP publishes selection opportunities, eligibility notes, results, and representative announcements for Indian players at international Go events.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-3 gap-5 my-8">
  <section class="official-card lg:col-span-2">
    <div class="official-card__body">
      <span class="official-card__meta">Selection Policy</span>
      <h2 class="official-card__title">How selection should work</h2>
      <p class="official-card__text">When AIGP receives an invitation or creates a national pathway, the selection information should be published as a News report or Event entry before the selection activity begins. It should name the event, available seats, eligibility rules, registration deadline, tournament format, time controls, tie-breaks, appeals process, and the contact person for questions.</p>
      <ul class="mt-5 space-y-2">
        <li>Selection tournaments should use clearly announced rules before play begins.</li>
        <li>Eligibility criteria and registration deadlines should be visible before registration closes.</li>
        <li>Pairings, results, withdrawals, disputes, and tie-breaks should be recorded for future audit.</li>
        <li>Final representative announcements should be published in News or Events and added to the representative register.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <aside class="official-card official-card--flat">
    <div class="official-card__body">
      <span class="official-card__meta">Need Help?</span>
      <h2 class="official-card__title">Appeals And Questions</h2>
      <p class="official-card__text">Players should first contact the organizer named in the News report or Event entry. For unresolved concerns, use the official contact route and include the event name, player name, round or ruling involved, screenshots, game records, and any supporting evidence.</p>
      <p class="mt-5"><a class="button button-primary" href="/contact-escalation/">Contact Us</a></p>
    </div>
  </aside>
</div>

<section class="site-section--tight">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <span class="section-eyebrow">Eligibility</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">Baseline Requirements</h2>
    <p class="section-summary">Each international event may add its own rules. These are the baseline expectations AIGP should make visible when opening a pathway.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card-grid card-grid--3">
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">Host Event Rules</h3><p class="official-card__text">Players may need to satisfy nationality, residency, age, passport, federation, amateur/professional, or rating requirements set by the event host.</p></div></div>
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">Active Player Record</h3><p class="official-card__text">Ratings, previous results, contact details, availability, and AIGP registration status may be used to verify participation.</p></div></div>
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">Representative Conduct</h3><p class="official-card__text">Representatives are expected to follow AIGP conduct rules, host event rules, fair-play expectations, and communication deadlines.</p></div></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="site-section--tight">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <span class="section-eyebrow">Selection Records</span>
    <h2 class="section-title">What Should Be Published</h2>
    <p class="section-summary">A transparent pathway should leave enough public record for players, parents, hosts, and future organizers to understand the outcome.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card-grid card-grid--3">
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">Before</h3><p class="official-card__text">Invitation details, seats available, eligibility, entry process, deadline, organizer contact, rules, and schedule.</p></div></div>
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">During</h3><p class="official-card__text">Pairings, results, tie-break standings, forfeits, withdrawals, and any official rulings that affect selection.</p></div></div>
    <div class="official-card"><div class="official-card__body"><h3 class="official-card__title">After</h3><p class="official-card__text">Representative names, event links, source News report or Event entry, final standings, and later event reports where available.</p></div></div>
  </div>
</section>
