# Privacy Policy

AIGP collects only the information needed to operate the association website, respond to messages, manage memberships, run events, and share community updates.

## Information We May Collect

- Contact details submitted through forms.
- Membership form details such as name, email, and playing level.
- Event registration details submitted through linked third-party services.
- Basic analytics data used to understand website usage.

## Third-Party Services

This site may use services such as Mailchimp, Formspree, Google reCAPTCHA, GoatCounter, YouTube, Facebook, Google Sheets, and Leago. These services may process data according to their own policies.

## How Information Is Used

- To respond to enquiries.
- To send membership or event updates.
- To administer tournaments, workshops, and community activities.
- To improve the website and public information.

## Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, use the [Contact](/contact/) page.
