# Code Of Conduct

AIGP aims to maintain a welcoming, fair, and respectful environment for Go players, learners, teachers, organizers, parents, schools, and guests.

## Expected Conduct

- Treat other players, organizers, teachers, and volunteers with respect.
- Play fairly and follow tournament rules, platform rules, and organizer instructions.
- Keep teaching and review spaces constructive, especially for beginners and children.
- Respect privacy when sharing photos, game records, contact details, or screenshots.
- Report issues through the appropriate organizer or contact channel.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Harassment, threats, abuse, hate speech, or discriminatory behavior.
- Cheating, sandbagging, impersonation, or knowingly submitting false results.
- Publishing private contact details or personal information without permission.
- Disrupting events, classes, meetings, or official online spaces.

## Reporting

Concerns can be sent through the [Contact](/contact/) page. For urgent or sensitive concerns, follow the [Contact and Escalation Policy](/contact-escalation/).
