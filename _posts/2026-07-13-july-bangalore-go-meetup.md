The **Bangalore Go Game Meetup** on **12th July 2026** was another fun gathering for the Indian **Go (Baduk/Weiqi)** community. Players of all levels joined in for friendly games, game reviews, and plenty of Go discussions with fellow board game enthusiasts, and enjoy the timeless game of Go in a welcoming environment. If you're looking for a **Go club in Bangalore**, want to **learn Go**, or simply enjoy **strategy board games**, our regular **Bangalore Go meetups** are the perfect place to start. We look forward to seeing even more players at the next meetup!
