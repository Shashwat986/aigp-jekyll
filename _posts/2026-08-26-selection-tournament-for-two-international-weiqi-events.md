The **Association of Indian Go Players (AIGP)** will conduct an online selection tournament on **30 August 2026 at 5:00 PM IST** on **Online Go Server (OGS)** to select Indian representatives for two upcoming international Weiqi events.

## Events

### 1. Korean Ambassadors Cup

Main Event will be held in **Incheon, Republic of Korea**, from **6–13 November 2026**.

This is one of the major international amateur Baduk championships, bringing together representatives from Go associations around the world.

The championship includes:

* **National Division**
* **Youth Division (U15)**

### 2. Weiqi Festival – China

This event will be held in **Qidong City, Jiangsu Province, China**, from **22–26 October 2026**.

The international competition includes an **Overseas Group** for players representing countries and regions outside mainland China, with separate Men's and Women's rankings.

## AIGP Selection Tournament

**Date:** Sunday, 30 August 2026
**Time:** 5:00 PM IST
**Platform:** Online Go Server (OGS)

The tournament will be used by AIGP to select India's representatives for the above international events.

Players interested in representing **India internationally** are encouraged to participate.

Further details regarding registration, tournament format, pairings and selection procedure will be announced by AIGP.

We wish all participants the very best and look forward to seeing India's Go community compete for the opportunity to represent the country on the international stage.

\*AIGP will provide financial assistance to cover a portion of the associated costs.
