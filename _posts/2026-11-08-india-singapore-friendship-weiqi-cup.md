**Event Details -**

1. **Format**: 10v10 pitting teams from India and Singapore against each other.
2. **Fair Play**: Please note that there will be no anti-cheating measures implemented, as this event is a friendly exchange conducted in good faith. 
3. **Online Platforms**: We will be using Google Meet for Ceremonies. We will be using Online Go Server for the matches.

**Agenda (Tournament Date 12-13 September 2026 - 2 Days Saturday/Sunday) -** 

1. **Opening Ceremony (Saturday 11:30am IST; 2 pm SGT)**: A 30-minute ceremony to welcome all
participants and set the stage for the event, everyone should join the Google Meet.
2. **First Round (Saturday 12pm IST; 2:30 pm SGT)**: Matches will commence immediately after the
opening ceremony.
3. **Second Round (Saturday 2pm IST; 4:30 pm SGT)**: Matches will commence immediately after
the pairings are done.
4. **Third Round (Sunday 12pm IST; 2:30 pm SGT)**: DDK matches as usual and SDKs play Rengo.
5. **Fourth Round (Sunday 2pm IST; 4:30 pm SGT)**: Matches will commence immediately after the pairings are done.
6﻿. **Closing Ceremony (4:30pm IST; 7:00 pm SGT)**: A 30-minute ceremony where we congratulate
all winners, and end the session, along with dignitaries from both ends.

**Tournament Details -** 

There will be 10 players from each country, split into three brackets:

* Bracket 1: Two players ranked 5 - 9 kyu
* Bracket 2: Four players ranked 10 - 14 kyu
* Bracket 3: Four players ranked 15-20 Kyu
