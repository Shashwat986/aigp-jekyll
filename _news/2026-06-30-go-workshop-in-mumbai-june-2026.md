**Go (Baduk/Weiqi) workshop in Mumbai, India.** 

Introducing participants to one of the world's oldest and most strategic board games. The workshop covered Go basics, strategy, tactical reading, and guided gameplay in a welcoming, hands-on learning environment. It was a unique opportunity to learn the **Game of Go in India**, connect with the growing **Go community in Mumbai**, and experience the rich tradition of this timeless strategy game.
