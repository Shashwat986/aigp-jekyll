During the promotion of the **Go game in Hingoli district**, students at **Global Public School** were introduced to the ancient strategy board game through both **offline workshops** and **online sessions**. The interactive demonstrations helped students quickly understand the basics of Go, and they showed great enthusiasm while playing. The event encouraged logical thinking, concentration, creativity, and problem-solving skills, making the **Go game** an exciting educational activity for young learners in Hingoli.
