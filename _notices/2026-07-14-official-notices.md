date: "2026-07-14"
description: AIGP official notices will be published separately from general news for easier discovery and record keeping.

  - Association Update

AIGP official notices are organized under the available categories: Association Update, Rating, Membership, Policy, Partners, and Financial. Selection tournaments and national representation announcements should use Association Update, while rules and process changes should use Policy.

General community updates and event reports will continue to appear in the news section.
